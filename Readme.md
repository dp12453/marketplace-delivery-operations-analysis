# Marketplace Delivery Reliability & Seller Operations Analysis

**Project Status: In Progress**

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing e-commerce delivery operations using the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset.

The objective is to understand delivery performance, identify operational challenges, evaluate seller performance, and investigate the relationship between delivery experience and customer satisfaction.

## Business Objectives

- Measure on-time and late delivery performance.
- Analyse delivery delays across sellers and product categories.
- Evaluate shipping and freight costs.
- Understand the relationship between delivery performance and customer reviews.
- Identify operational areas that may require improvement.

## Dataset

**Source:** [Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)

The dataset contains information about orders, customers, sellers, products, payments, reviews, and delivery operations.

## Tools & Technologies

- SQL – Data querying and business analysis
